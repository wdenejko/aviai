Why it matters Unit conversion isn't a scaling problem — it's a capability none of these models have, confirmed across five. That makes it the clean finetuning lever, and it's missing at every size. A small model that learned the ×33.86 and ×1.94 mappings could jump disproportionately.
03 — The tiny end

Two ways to fail at ~0.5B

Same size class, opposite personalities — and the family, not the size, decides which.

Reckless

Gemma 3 270M

  • hallucination 67% — fabricates a value for almost every absent field
  • JSON-valid 69% — often can't even format
  • value-acc 17% — confident and wrong
Conservative

Qwen2.5 0.5B

  • abstains on ~60% of fields — mostly emits null
  • hallucination 1% · JSON-valid 99%
  • value-acc 5% — safe and empty

For a safety domain, Qwen's silence beats Gemma-270M's confident fabrication. Hallucination otherwise shrinks with Gemma size — 67% → 20% → 10% → 8% — so bigger Gemma is also safer Gemma.

04 — Stack

The serving stack is worth ~9 points

The same E4B scores 69% here (dashi · llama.cpp · Q8_0) versus 77.8% on the M5 (MLX · 4-bit). Q8 is higher fidelity than 4-bit, so this isn't quantization — it's the stack, most likely llama.cpp's Gemma-3n implementation (a tricky Per-Layer-Embeddings / MatFormer architecture) being less faithful than MLX, or a chat-template difference.

Consequence A paired before/after must never cross stacks. Phase 4 trains and evaluates on dashi, so the anchor is the same-stack 69%, not 77.8%. Re-running the baseline here was the right call.
05 — Phase 4

What the sweep hands the finetune

  • Confirmed lever: LoRA the unit conversions — the gap is universal, so the target is real at every size.
  • Richer question: does finetuning help more at small or large sizes? Can a finetuned 1–2B reach base-4B decode? A small model that gains conversion and sheds the reckless hallucination is the on-device story worth chasing.
  • Success criterion: beat the E4B anchor (69% value-acc, 4% EM) without raising the 8% hallucination, verified by a McNemar paired test on the same 620 records.
